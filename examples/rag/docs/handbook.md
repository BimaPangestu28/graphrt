# Employee handbook

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Outside core hours, schedule your own time.

## Leave
Every employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Unused leave carries over for 6 months.
Sick leave does not count against annual leave and needs a doctor's note after 3 consecutive days.

## Expenses
Expenses below 500 USD are approved by your manager. Anything above 500 USD needs finance approval.
Submit receipts within 30 days.
